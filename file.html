About Connect

A locally family-owned electrical business operating out of Mount Gambier, SA.

Family-Owned. Locally Operated. Trusted.

Connect Electrical SE was built on a simple idea — deliver quality electrical work with honest communication and fast turnaround. As a family-owned business, we take pride in every job, big or small.

From new home builds and renovations to large-scale solar and battery systems, our team brings the skills, experience, and reliability that the Limestone Coast depends on. We respond within 1-2 hours and get the job done right, first time.

Whether it's a switchboard upgrade in Mount Gambier or a full off-grid install on a rural property — we're connecting you.
